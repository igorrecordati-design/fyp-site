FAQ

Do I need to be an athlete?

No. Every session is scaled to your level, from beginners to competitive athletes.

Can I mix indoor and outdoor group sessions?

Yes. One group pack covers both.

Do I get access to a training app?

Yes. Your program lives in the app, with a video demo for every exercise. Every session, set and weight you log is saved, so you can track your progressions over time and see exactly how far you've come.
