Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Forever Young Performance (“FYP”, “I”, “me”) is a strength & conditioning coaching business based in Sydney, Australia, run by Igor Recordati. This policy explains what personal information I collect through this website, why, where it is stored and how long I keep it.

What I collect

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone / WhatsApp number and city.
  • Training information: the program you're interested in, goals, training experience, availability, sport, training location and equipment.
  • Health information: any injuries, physical limitations or pain you choose to tell me about. I only collect health information with your consent, which you give when you submit the application form.
  • Anything else you choose to share in the application form.
  • Waitlist: if you join the Blueprints waitlist, your email address.

Why I collect it

  • To assess your application and decide which program fits you.
  • To design and adjust your training, taking any injuries and limitations into account.
  • To contact you about your application and your training.
  • To let you know when Blueprints launch, if you joined the waitlist.

Where it is stored

  • Netlify: this website is hosted on Netlify, and applications and waitlist sign-ups are stored there through Netlify Forms.
  • Gmail: a copy of each submission is sent by email to my Gmail address.
  • The training app I use for programs: if you become a client, the information needed to build and deliver your program is stored in the training app I use for programs.

These services may store data on servers outside Australia.

How long I keep it

  • If you don't become a client, I keep your application for up to 12 months and then delete it.
  • If you become a client, I keep your information for up to 7 years.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, by emailing igor@forever-young-performance.com.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how your information has been handled, please email me first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Cookies

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit.

Changes to this policy

The latest version of this policy will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
