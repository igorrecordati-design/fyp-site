Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Forever Young Performance (“FYP”, “I”, “me”) is a strength & conditioning coaching business based in Sydney, Australia, run by Igor Recordati. This policy explains what personal information I collect, why, how it is stored and shared, and the choices you have. I handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

What I collect

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone / WhatsApp number and city.
  • Training information: the program you're interested in, goals, training experience, availability, sport, training location and equipment.
  • Health information: any injuries, physical limitations or pain you choose to tell me about. This is “sensitive information” under the Privacy Act, so I only collect it with your consent.
  • Anything else you choose to share in the application form, by email, WhatsApp or Instagram.
  • Waitlist: if you join the Blueprints waitlist, your email address.

You can choose not to share health information, but I may then be unable to design a safe, appropriate program for you.

Why I collect it

  • To assess your application and decide which program fits you.
  • To design, deliver and adjust your training safely, taking injuries and limitations into account.
  • To contact you about your application, sessions, scheduling and billing.
  • To let you know when Blueprints launch, if you joined the waitlist.

I don't sell your information and I don't use your health information for marketing.

How it is stored and who can see it

Only I (Igor Recordati) have access to your application. To run the website and deliver coaching I use a small number of service providers, who store or process information on my behalf:

  • Netlify, which hosts this website and receives form submissions. Netlify stores data on servers that may be located outside Australia, including in the United States.
  • My email provider, which receives a copy of each submission.
  • The training app used to deliver your program, if you become a client.

Because some of these providers store data overseas, your information may be disclosed outside Australia. I take reasonable steps to use reputable providers with appropriate security. I will only otherwise disclose your information if you agree, or if the law requires or allows it (for example, in an emergency involving a serious threat to your health or safety).

How long I keep it

If you don't become a client, I delete your application within 12 months. If you train with me, I keep your information while you're a client and for up to 7 years afterwards for record-keeping, then delete it. You can ask me to delete it sooner (see below).

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, by emailing igor@forever-young-performance.com. I'll respond within 30 days.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how your information has been handled, please email me first and I'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with my response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Cookies and analytics

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
