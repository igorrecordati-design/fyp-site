About the mission

Forever young.

Forever Young Performance is a Sydney strength & conditioning practice for people who want to perform, move and feel well for life — from beginners to competitive athletes. One-to-one, in small groups, on the basketball court and online.

Founder & head coach

Igor Recordati

Personal Trainer & Sports Coach · Sydney · 6+ years

Born in Milan, Italy — with Croatian roots and a career that took him through the Philippines, China and South Korea — Igor brings a genuinely global perspective to performance training. That breadth of experience across different cultures and industries is exactly what makes him understand people: where they're coming from, what they're juggling, and what they actually need.

Now based in Sydney, Igor works with individuals of all ages and backgrounds — from beginners to competitive athletes — helping them build real strength, restore athleticism, and move and feel better for life. His approach is direct: heavy lifting done right, movement quality developed through precise and scalable drills, and programs designed to deliver results even with a packed schedule.

The philosophy

The name isn't a wish. It's a practice.

The name comes from the Japanese idea of Tokowaka — 常若. Toko means constant, everlasting; waka means young, vital. Together they describe not a state you arrive at, but one you continuously choose.

The phoenix carries the same truth. It doesn't survive by staying the same — it survives by burning down and rising again. Perpetual reinvention. Resilience built through renewal, not resistance to change.

This is what Forever Young Performance is built on. Training here isn't about chasing a number on a scale or a one-rep max. It draws from yoga and animal movement patterns, old-school lifts and athletic conditioning, agility work and breath — because the body that lasts is the one that can do many things well, and recover from all of them.

Every session is a small act of renewal. That's the work. That's the philosophy. Forever Young.
